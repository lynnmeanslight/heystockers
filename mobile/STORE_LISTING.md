## Full description

HeyStockers combines non-custodial stock trading with a trade-verified social reputation layer.

Browse the highest-volume tokenized stocks on Solana, preview a live route, and approve every buy or sell inside your own wallet. Your assets remain in your wallet throughout the experience.

Create a username, follow other traders, and publish position calls with a target, deadline, and commitment. A position call becomes public only after its matching trade is verified onchain, so rejecting the wallet transaction creates no post.

Key features:

- Buy tokenized stocks with USDC and sell supported holdings.
- See real supported balances from the connected wallet.
- Review live routes before approving in your wallet.
- Publish position calls backed by confirmed trades.
- Search usernames, follow traders, signal calls, and invite friends.
- Delete your off-chain account and social activity from inside the app.

Tokenized assets involve market, liquidity, issuer, smart-contract, and regulatory risk. HeyStockers does not provide investment advice. Network and market costs may apply.
